Evangelion Battle Mission (ヱヴァンゲリヲン バトルミッション, Wevangerion Batoru Misshon?), (also known as EVA BM (ヱヴァBM?) was a smartphone game based on studio Khara inc.'s Rebuild of Evangelion film series. It was licensed by Khara, and developed by Donuts Co., Ltd. And published by BushiroadWP. The game had its service terminated on 21 January 2016.[1]

Plot[]

The "Second Impact" is an unprecedented disaster that suddenly struck the world. Nearly half of humanity died as a result of this, and traces of great destruction were left in many parts of the world.

A 14-year-old boy, Shinji Ikari, is shown a gigantic humanoid weapon called "Evangelion" developed in secret by his father, Gendo Ikari, and is forced to fight against a gigantic creature of unknown identity called the "Angel". He was forced to do so.

Then, during the battle with the many apostles, he met the pilot of Evangelion Unit-00, "Rei Ayanami", the pilot of Evangelion Unit-02, "Asuka Shikinami Langley", and the pilot of Evangelion Provisional Unit-05, "Mari Makinami Illustrious".[2]
Gameplay[]

The battle system is an easy-to-operate puzzle game where you connect blocks of the same color and use combos to defeat the attacking Angels in real time. All illustrations, including famous scenes from the original work and original situations, were newly drawn, and new illustrations were added every month.[3]
